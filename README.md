A short text for the AI judge, spoken in the second person: you keep a stone in your pocket, warm from the day you picked it up, and your hand finds it again and again — the same weight, the same shape — while the road and the wind keep coming and the stone simply stays. Nothing tells you to be brave; the steadiness of one small, ordinary thing does the work.
